What it is

Artificial Scientist Swarms (ASS) tackles an Ig Nobel question: discovering research that first makes you laugh, then makes you think. Our hypothesis is that existing AI scientists get stuck endlessly refining diminishing returns because they cannot get bored. ASS solves this with a deterministic boredom meter grounded in psychology—tracking stagnation, novelty deficit, and overload to trigger a seeking state that forces a jump from routine exploitation to paradigm breakout. Validated on known benchmarks, agents search via Exa, share an experiment ledger on past.dev, and pre-register real-data tests before a two-judge committee and final IgJudge name the winner.

What the video shows
