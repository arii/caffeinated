Social Jetlag: The Hangover Your Pancreas Pays For propose
A preregistered triangulation study asking whether social jetlag is merely a glamorous name for Monday-morning physiological spite, or a real circadian-metabolic exposure. I have not loaded a dataset yet; the test would use CDC/NHANES 2017-March 2020 pre-pandemic public files for the phenotype arm and the public 697,828-person chronotype GWAS summary statistics underlying [S11], plus public outcome GWAS summary statistics indexed in GWAS Catalog for BMI/adiposity, fasting glucose, lipids and type 2 diabetes, for the genetic-triangulation arm.
hypothesis & method

In non-shift adults, greater social jetlag will track higher metabolic-syndrome risk only weakly and easily confabulated overall, as hinted by the overall-null NHANES 2017-2020 pattern in [S4]; the signal should be clearer when evening preference, weekend oversleep, depressive liability and metabolic risk line up. Genetically instrumented evening chronotype, carrying the chronotype architecture and depression correlations reported in [S11], should show directionally coherent Mendelian-randomization estimates for metabolic-syndrome components rather than reverse causation.

Arm 1, phenotypic epidemiology: download public NHANES 2017-March 2020; restrict to non-pregnant adults who are not in reported shift/night-work categories; derive workday/free-day sleep midpoint and social jetlag from sleep-timing items, and harmonized metabolic syndrome from waist, blood pressure, HDL cholesterol, triglycerides and fasting glucose in the fasting subsample. Use survey-weighted logistic and linear models with Taylor design, adjusting for age, sex, race/ethnicity, poverty income ratio, smoking, alcohol, total sleep time, physical activity and depressive symptom screen. Pre-specify strata: earlier vs later sleep-midpoint chronotype proxy, obese metabolically healthy vs unhealthy phenotypes as in [S1], Hispanic/Latino subgroup comparison against the HCHS/SOL question in [S5], and consistency checks against the positive Japanese working-population signal in [S2]. Arm 2, genetic triangulation: obtain public chronotype GWAS summary statistics from [S11], clump independent instruments, then test SNP-to-metabolic-outcome effects using public GWAS Catalog outcome summary statistics; run inverse-variance weighted MR with weighted-median, MR-Egger and MR-PRESSO sensitivity checks, Steiger directionality filtering, F-statistic instrument-strength screening, heterogeneity tests and false-discovery-rate control. Triangulate rather than merely correlate: require concordance of NHANES adjusted phenotype, Japanese/cohort direction in [S1,S2,S5], and MR direction, while explicitly treating the NHANES overall null in [S4] as evidence to be explained, not erased. Falsification features: adjustment for total sleep time, weekend catch-up sleep as a mediator, negative-control exposures such as non-sleep-timing leisure behavior, negative-control outcomes where plausible, and null reporting with confidence intervals if coherence fails. No data would be invented; every estimate must be reproducible from public files.

The Rotten Egg in the Sugar Bowl: Do H2S-Miller Spark Products Short-Circuit the Formose Route to Ribose? propose
Test whether sulfur-bearing amines/amino acids characteristic of the archived 1958 Miller H2S-rich spark-discharge mixture change the competition among formaldehyde consumption, glycolaldehyde autocatalysis, and ribose formation. The laughable premise is that the Primordial Soup may have needed a little brimstone mischief to keep it honest; the serious premise is that trace sulfur electrophile/radical scavengers could redirect a network normally rich in aldoses toward dead-end adducts. No dataset is loaded yet, so the test would use real public data rather than invented measurements: the published absolute/relative amino-acid yield tables and HPLC-MS findings from the H2S Miller archive work [S17], the conserved yields-relative-to-glycine pattern across spark hydrocarbon runs [S16], the published Formose-network conditions and ribose-capable aldose/ketose chemistry in water with Ca2+ [S21], and the measured catalysis/competition behavior of the core glycolaldehyde cycle [S22].
hypothesis & method

Reconstructed H2S-rich spark-product mixtures, normalized to the S16/S17 glycine-relative yield pattern and added at trace prebiotic levels to formaldehyde/glycolaldehyde/Ca2+ Formose mixtures, will trap carbonyl intermediates and quench radical-like formose propagation enough to reduce ribose accumulation and delay/shorten the autocatalytic glycolaldehyde acceleration, relative to a sulfur-free hydrocarbon-spark reconstruction. Sulfur-free systems should show the S22-like acceleration in C1 food consumption; H2S-enriched systems should show lower ribose-to-total-aldose selectivity and more sulfur-containing adducts by HPLC-MS.

Build two matched abiotic reaction-network panels in buffered water with Ca2+: (1) sulfur-free spark reconstruction using published hydrocarbon+NH3 yield ratios normalized to glycine [S16]; (2) H2S-rich reconstruction using the reported H2S spark product classes normalized to glycine [S17]. Run formaldehyde feeds seeded vs unseeded with glycolaldehyde across time, at least in triplicate, randomizing run order. Profile mixtures by HPLC-MS; assign diagnostic structures by NMR where isolated; confirm amino/amine loading by GC-MS after derivatization. Quantify formaldehyde decay, glycolaldehyde/C2 intermediate build-up and decay, total aldoses/ketoses, ribose specifically, and sulfur-adduct byproducts. Reconstruct the network with published Formose yields/kinetics [S21,S22] and fit whether added sulfur terms are required to explain the data better than a no-sulfur network. Real public data to test it: supplementary/tabulated yields and analyses in S16 and S17 and the network conditions/yield data in S21/S22; any archived-sample material would be used only if formally shared by its custodians, not assumed. Primary readout is ribose concentration at matched formaldehyde conversion.

The Swarm That Cannot Make Up Its Mind: Critical Slowing Down Before Bacterial Turbulence propose
Treat confined bacterial swarms near the onset of collective motion as a system approaching a decision, not merely as faster flow. Vortex reversal and spontaneous oscillations reported for confined bacteria are reframed as measurable indecision: the nematic/vortex state hesitates, flips, and hesitates again before full turbulence. The joke is that bacteria may announce a phase transition by being unable to commit; the think is whether public movies contain enough statistics to detect early-warning signals rather than just pretty swirls.
hypothesis & method

As confinement and density move a bacterial suspension toward the transition documented in emergence/confined-well studies, order-parameter relaxation time, low-frequency vorticity power, and autocorrelation time increase, and the vortex-reversal precursor becomes more frequent and more strongly correlated with the subsequent loss of stable vortex motion. This is a positive, pre-transitional slowing-down signature rather than a claim that total swim speed must rise. No dataset is loaded yet; the test would use published accessible materials only.

Reanalyze real public data: the experimental videos/phase-diagram materials accompanying the Science Advances emergence study [S31], confined cylindrical-well phenomenology from [S34] and [S42], and scale-free collective-motion context from [S44]; interpret any linear-response feedback through [S45]. Pipeline: track/PIV or PTV on published supplementary videos where available; estimate vorticity, nematic order parameter, kinetic energy spectra, MSD/VACF-style persistence, and windowed autocorrelation; sweep the control parameter reported in each study (confinement radius, density, or time since loading) and fit an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck/linear-response relaxation model by Bayesian inversion to extract relaxation time and low-frequency gain. Model comparison: with vs without memory/hydrodynamic feedback; controls: time-permutation nulls, frame-rate and illumination matched subsets, drift correction, inter-video hierarchical uncertainty. Report whether vortex-reversal rate predicts later transition better than mean speed alone. Effect sizes are targets for this new study, not results already observed.

Brimstone in the Sugar Bowl: Do H2S-Miller Spark Products Reroute Formaldehyde Away from Ribose? refine

Serious question: do trace sulfur-bearing amines/amino acids characteristic of the archived 1958 H2S Miller spark-discharge mixture act as carbonyl, Ca2+-chelating, or radical-scavenging sinks that change the competition among formaldehyde consumption, glycolaldehyde autocatalysis, and ribose-capable aldose/ketose formation? The refined study treats sulfur species not as flavor but as competitors for the same scarce C1/C2 intermediates and metal catalysts, testing whether they convert a promising sugar economy into dead-end adducts.
hypothesis & method

If Miller-relevant sulfur-bearing products are present at trace but nonzero levels, formaldehyde may be consumed at similar or even faster apparent rates while productive formose throughput falls: free glycolaldehyde amplification weakens, Ca2+-assisted aldol propagation toward pentoses/ribose is blunted, and carbon flux is redirected into sulfur-containing hemithioacetal/thioacetal/imine/Strecker-like or other non-formose sinks. The effect should be strongest at early network turns and near thiol:Ca2+/C1 trace ratios, become non-linear rather than simply proportional, and should not be interpreted as proof that H2S spark chemistry makes ribose; the alternative is modest radical cleanup without major flux diversion.

No dataset is loaded and no measurements are reported here. This would be tested only with real public data plus, optionally later, targeted validation: (1) extract the published HPLC-MS identification and absolute/relative amino-acid yield tables from the archived H2S-rich Miller experiment [S17]; use [S16] only as a glycine-normalized cross-run sanity pattern for spark hydrocarbon/H2S amino-acid products, not as a new measurement. (2) Use [S21] to reconstruct the water/Ca2+ aldose-ketose network that can make ribose and related products from formaldehyde via glycolaldehyde, and use [S22] as the measured kinetic backbone for the core CH2O -> glycolaldehyde -> higher aldoses cycle, including catalysis and competition. Global sensitivity analysis must rank sulfur identity and concentration against Ca2+ and initial glycolaldehyde. Validation means reproducing the published S17 product pattern and S16 glycine-normalized conservation, remaining directionally consistent with S21/S22, and only then proposing HPLC-MS/GC-MS/NMR bench tests with spiked sulfur species; until then, do not claim a ribose yield.

The bacteria are not indecisive; the order parameter is refine
Reframe confined bacterial swarms near onset not as simply flowing faster but as a decision system approaching commitment: persistent vortex motion gives way to periodic reversal and oscillation before developed turbulence, as reported for confined E. coli in cylindrical wells [S34] and for long-range feedback/oscillation models of confined swarms [S45]. The joke is that the swarm announces a phase transition by refusing to commit; the serious claim is that published movies may contain enough temporal statistics to test early-warning signals, not merely pretty swirls. This is constrained by the observed emergence/phase-diagram work [S31], triggered transition kinetics [S43], and the caution from scale-free collective motion [S44]: spatial correlation length may be unhelpful, so the decisive evidence must be dynamical and temporal.
hypothesis & method

Before the confinement-controlled sequence of persistent vortex circulation to periodic reversal/oscillation to turbulent flow [S34], and before or during speed-triggered emergence [S31, S43], the swarm should show critical-slowing-down-like signatures: rising variance of angular momentum/nematic order, increasing lag-1 autocorrelation or decreasing recovery rate, low-frequency power near the reversal/oscillation frequency predicted by feedback/Jeffery orientation theory [S45], and intermittent flickering between vortex-sign or nematic-alignment states. Null model: those fluctuations are only consequences of scale-free intermittent turbulence [S44] or imaging/tracking noise and carry no predictive information beyond local density and speed.

Do not generate new bacteria; reanalyze public material. Step 1: archive provenance from the Science Advances emergence papers and supplementary materials [S31, S43] and from confined-reversal/oscillation studies [S34, S45], checking data licenses and movie frame rates; where files are absent, email authors rather than invent numbers. Step 2: apply PIV/PTV-style reanalysis to published videos to extract velocity fields and centers of vorticity; construct Q-tensor/nematic director, net circulation, vortex-core number, vortex-sign lifetime, reversal interval, and local speed as low-dimensional order parameters. Step 3: compute rolling-window early-warning statistics: variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, return rate after spontaneous sign changes, spectral power near oscillation frequency and phase lag consistent with the [S45] phase-leading nematic response, skewness/flickering, and entropy of vortex-sign switches. Step 4: use Bayesian hierarchical/survival models to compare pre-reversal/pre-oscillation/pre-turbulence windows against matched reversed null windows and against far-from-transition controls, with paper, well radius, frame rate, illumination, cell density proxies, and tracking uncertainty as covariates. Step 5: preregister cross-dataset validation: train thresholds on one dataset/well series, test on another; report calibration and false-alarm rate, not only pretty phase portraits. Include negative controls and block bootstrap over wells to handle non-independent frames.

Rush Hour Robins: Does Traffic Impose a Musical Frequency Fence on the Dawn Chorus? propose
A comic-but-serious test of whether city birds are not merely singing earlier, as commonly suspected, but are squeezing their dawn song into the acoustic holes left by low-frequency traffic rumble. The laugh is birds filing their songs through a noise-canceling keyhole; the think is whether anthropogenic masking is a quantifiable selective pressure on song structure, distinct from artificial light.
hypothesis & method

Across comparable urban dawn recordings, higher anthropogenic low-frequency noise is associated with higher passerine song peak/centroid frequencies and reduced low-frequency song energy, after controlling for urban light, latitude/season, time relative to civil dawn, recorder quality, and species identity; this should be stronger than the light-associated explanation for timing shifts.

Use real public data only: CC-licensed time- and location-stamped dawn recordings from xeno-canto and Cornell Macaulay Library, filtered to within about 60 minutes of civil dawn using standard solar-time calculation; match each recording to public covariates from OpenStreetMap road density/class, ESA WorldCover or equivalent urban mask, VIIRS nighttime lights for artificial light, and modeled noise/NDSI-style low-vs-bird band energy where audio allows. Run BirdNET-style open call detection/classification with confidence thresholds, then extract spectrogram features and acoustic indices including NDSI and ACI. Model detection-weighted song frequency features with mixed-effects models: frequency ~ low-frequency noise + VIIRS light + urban cover + latitude*doy + minutes-from-civil-dawn + (1|species) + (1|city/site) + recorder quality, with detection covariates and repeated-site occupancy/detection logic rather than naive presence counts. Add negative controls (midday recordings; high-frequency insect/bird bands) and sensitivity analyses for citizen-science bias. No dataset has been loaded yet; this would be a preregistered analysis of public archives, not a claim of completed results.

The Bicarbonate Bodyguard: Does Mineral Water Disarm the Sonic Bubble? propose
A lab study with a joke in the title and teeth in the design: ask whether the bicarbonate that makes mineral water taste 'healthy' also acts as a microscopic bodyguard for pollutants, soaking up hydroxyl radicals generated by acoustic cavitation. The laugh lands first, then the uneasy chemistry: your refreshing drink may be a terrible advanced oxidation reactor.
hypothesis & method

Relative to deionized water, bicarbonate-rich mineral-like matrices will show lower sonochemical oxidant flux and slower loss of a model organic pollutant, because bicarbonate scavenges cavitation-derived hydroxyl radicals before they reach the target. This extends the review finding that bicarbonate-rich water suppresses ultrasound pollutant elimination after treatment versus deionized water.

No dataset is loaded. Run a randomized horn-reactor study using standard high-intensity ultrasonic probe calorimetry; deionized vs controlled alkalinity matrices matched by pH and conductivity (e.g., 0, 50, 150, 300 mg/L alkalinity as CaCO3), with N2-sparged controls informed by EPR radical speciation. Measure sonochemical oxidant flux by KI oxidation dosimetry and Fricke-style ferrous oxidation, confirm transient radicals by DMPO spin-trap EPR, and track a model pollutant and byproducts by UV-Vis plus LC-MS/GC-MS under fixed acoustic power, temperature, volume, and treatment time. Pre-register the dose-response and use mixed-effects models with reactor as random effect. Public-data test/benchmark: use the Water Quality Portal / USGS National Water Information System and EPA STORET alkalinity, pH, total dissolved solids, and hardness records to choose environmentally realistic bicarbonate regimes and weight external-validity estimates; use reported pollutant-removal trends from the urine/seawater/mineral-water sonochemistry review [S75] as benchmark direction, not as raw outcome data. Direct public datasets for sonochemical radical yields likely do not exist, so the causal claim must come from the lab experiment; public water-chemistry data only test ecological realism and generalizability.

The Water-Jacket Hustle: Do haze aerosols game Köhler theory at 90-98% RH? propose
Use near-activation hygroscopic growth as a 'tell' for which urban and background aerosols are secretly hoarding a water jacket before they opt out and become cloud droplets. The joke is that pollution particles behave like they smuggle water into cloud base; the think is whether high-RH HTDMA residuals improve CCN prediction beyond dry size and ordinary growth factors.
hypothesis & method

For accumulation/Aitken-mode aerosols, the slope of growth-factor increase between 90% and 98% RH carries extra curvature/Raoult information: after controlling for dry diameter and lower-RH growth, particles with a steeper high-RH climb will activate at lower supersaturation than Köhler-only predictions from bulk composition imply. This follows from temperature-stabilized high-RH HTDMA feasibility [S84], the Kelvin-versus-Raoult framing [S78], closure toward cloud droplet formation [S79], and published method/site differences in size-resolved growth during haze and at four Thai sites [S81, S82].

No dataset is loaded yet; the study would request public near-surface aerosol records from ACTRIS/EBAS where HTDMA growth factors, SMPS size distributions/CPC counts and CCN measurements are co-located or tightly time-matched by station; DOE ARM public CCN and size-distribution products would be a cross-check where HTDMA is absent. Analysis: harmonize reported growth factors to common dry mobility diameters (e.g., 50, 100, 150, 200 nm), fit a fixed k-Köhler/HTDMA curve using sub-90% RH growth plus dry size, then compute residual high-RH behavior from 90-98% RH where available [S84]. Regress CCN concentration at supersaturations roughly 0.2-0.6% on residual high-RH slope plus controls (dry D50/N, temperature, RH, season/site, haze indicator). Quantify out-of-sample gain via cross-validation and compare against a Köhler-only model rooted in [S78, S79]. Tianjin haze and Thailand four-site findings [S81, S82] are used only as published priors/sanity checks, not as raw data unless the underlying public files are obtainable. Key limitation: EBAS-style archives may be heterogeneous in RH setpoints and uncertainty, so the test is a confirmatory meta-analysis of public records, not a new instrument campaign.
