Welcome to the lab.

Walk with WASD, sprint with Shift, and look with the mouse. Click the world to capture the mouse; press Esc to release it. You can also drag to look or use the arrow keys. Approach a station and press E to interact.

The numbered station buttons turn you toward your next destination. On touch screens, use the movement pad and drag the scene to look.

Replay mode (default)

Every number comes from a trace exported by export_trace.py from swarm artifacts (ledger, boredom log, Exa research log, shared memory, critic). Workers = culture bench, critic & conductor = analyzer, Exa scouts = expedition terminal, past.dev shared memory = notebook.

Tutorial mode: what does it simulate?

Two identical local optimizers search a synthetic growth landscape. When repeated experiments stop improving, the Boredom Meter switches one optimizer to a predefined alternate region. The baseline continues locally.

This is a scripted policy simulation, with synthetic scores and simulated research roles. The world is illustrative. No biological experiment or external AI service is running.
