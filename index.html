Agent identities, assignments, boredom, measurements and findings come from the backend. Reopening a run restores its persisted state. The room does not generate scores or decide what the researchers should do.

Protocol evaluation uses deterministic fake GPU scores and is labeled explicitly. Modal training produces real validation bits per byte. The central table visualizes team activity; it is not a biological simulation.
