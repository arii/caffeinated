THE ENGINE / FIELD GUIDE

A place for research.

Move with WASD, sprint with Shift, and look with your mouse. Click the scene to capture the pointer; Escape releases it. Drag or use arrow keys to look without capture. Approach a station and press E to inspect it.

Keys 1–4 turn you toward the experiment bench, analysis bench, explorer, or memory station. Switch between the four designs below to compare their layouts.

Built to connect.

The locked stack is Three.js, TypeScript, Vite, and Zod for event validation. Every room consumes the same research state and exposes the same station interface. A scientific solver or scheduler connects through a ResearchAdapter.

The current adapter is a labeled synthetic demonstration. Agent roles are simulated. No biological solver, external AI, or real experimental result is claimed. Live and replay adapters can use the same validated event contract.
